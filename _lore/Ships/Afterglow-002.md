A salvager, destroyed at [[Abernoth]] on 30 May 311 while making her slow retreat out of the system, five hours from the nearest warship. In little more than a minute, fire from eight energy guns that showed no hull crippled and destroyed her. Her commander, [[Laban Greenbrook]], was lost with her. Lifepods carried 78 of her 225 crew clear, and 147 died. She was the second ship lost to the ghosts of Abernoth, after [[Beacon 001]].
