The scarred moon of [[Tellane]]. An early Trans-Newtonian test scarred it in a near-miss, and the houses extended their old ban on war to TN weapons before anyone used them on each other. That amendment is the Compact.

The moon is tidally locked, so the scar always faces Tellane and hangs in its sky as a constant reminder. The Scar, the newest constellation, is drawn around the flash of the near-miss.
