The second world, named for the colour Tellani astronomers saw through their telescopes, centuries before anyone landed. Its thick atmosphere suggests white cloud tops. The trailblazer houses keep a name of their own for it: Kaukomere, "the far water".

Oskari, Dunmere and Thalmere paid to settle it by chemical rocket, a crossing that took months and was ruinously expensive. They hold claims here that Varenne, Ashgrove and Corvane never paid for. The world is two generations old. Its first native-born generation is now adult, knows [[Tellane]] only by radio and telescope, and is starting to see itself as a people of its own.

Trans-Newtonian technology makes the crossing routine. The trailblazers want their claims recognised as earned, the homeworld houses say the Council governs everything under the Compact, and the unclaimed regions are the first prize.
