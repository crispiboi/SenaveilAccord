The homeworld, from the oldest word in the common tongue for soil or ground. Its people are the Tellani.

All six great houses live here, and the Council keeps the Home Guard here. Varenne, Ashgrove and Corvane stayed home when the trailblazers paid to settle [[Alba]]. "Tellane-born" is becoming a pointed phrase.

Its moon, [[Witness]], carries the scar of the near-miss.
