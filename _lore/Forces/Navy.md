## The reform of 313

On 18 July 313 the Council reformed the navy. Two hunts in the home system had shown that six house squadrons, each moved only by Council mandate, could not answer an enemy that appears without warning and vanishes before the vote is counted. The six squadrons were dissolved into three commands under the Admiralty: the **Home Guard** to defend Tellane and Alba, **Strike Command** around the carriers, and the **Frontier Watch** to hold the jump points. Ships keep their houses' names and crews, and each house's crews serve together as a division within the commands.

Two conditions were written in. At Ashgrove's insistence, no command may fire first without the Chancery's assent. At Dunmere's, no house's crews are ever broken up among strangers.
