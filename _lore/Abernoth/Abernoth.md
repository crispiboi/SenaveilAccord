Two dim lamps, three planets, and a graveyard. Something hunts here, and the Accord now calls it the Hollow.

Found on 15 October 300 by SV-01 Driftmark under Sub-Lieutenant Herbert Whitsell, who was awarded the Wayfinder's Compass, Class III, for the discovery.

### The graveyard

At least 33 wrecks of unknown class lie in Abernoth, over 1.1 million tons between them, so others were here before us. The three largest are 119,531, 111,853 and 111,853 tons. The count comes from the visible contact list, which runs to #44, so more may lie hidden in the cluster. Wrecks of the same tonnages were later found at [[Quarrane]].

### Notable events

**13 May 311: the loss of Beacon 001.** An unseen ship armed with energy weapons attacked STB-01 [[Beacon 001]] at Abernoth. Three hits pierced her hull and crippled the stabilisation module, and the Beacon suffered a catastrophic failure and exploded. Her commander, [[Octavian Paccius]], was lost with the ship. Lifepods carried 54 of her 250 crew clear, and 196 died. It was the Accord's first ship lost to a hostile hand.

**30 May 311: the loss of Afterglow 002.** The 7th Abernoth Reaction Force came to hunt. The heavy cruiser Orvaux and the light cruiser Aubrac held the jump point where the Beacon died, while two strike groups swept the dark between the two jump points. They found nothing. The Hollow struck instead at the salvager [[Afterglow 002]], making her slow retreat out of the system 116 million km and five hours from the nearest warship. In little more than a minute of fire from eight energy guns that showed no hull, she was destroyed. Her commander, [[Laban Greenbrook]], was lost with her, and 147 of her 225 crew died. In seventeen days the Hollow cost the Accord two ships and 343 lives.

**27 June 311: the sweep fails.** The Reaction Force returned to Aurane having found nothing. The Council made sensor research the Lyceum's first priority, and until the realm could see its enemy, Abernoth was left to the Hollow.

**1 August 311: the Lyceum's finding.** The enemy is not invisible. It is small, and it sees before it is seen. The Yards were ordered to build new eyes, a great thermal sensor to catch a hunter's engines and an active sensor tuned for small craft, carried by ships that sail dark.
