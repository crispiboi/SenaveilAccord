Two dim lamps, three planets, and a graveyard.

At least 33 wrecks of unknown class lie in Abernoth, over 1.1 million tons between them, so others have been here before us. The three largest are 119,531, 111,853 and 111,853 tons. The count comes from the visible contact list, which runs to #44, so more may lie hidden in the cluster.

Found on 15 October 300 by SV-01 Driftmark under Sub-Lieutenant Herbert Whitsell, who was awarded the Wayfinder's Compass, Class III, for the discovery.
