Two dim lamps, three planets, and a graveyard.

At least 33 wrecks of unknown class lie in Abernoth, over 1.1 million tons between them, so others have been here before us. The three largest are 119,531, 111,853 and 111,853 tons. The count comes from the visible contact list, which runs to #44, so more may lie hidden in the cluster.

Found on 15 October 300 by SV-01 Driftmark under Sub-Lieutenant Herbert Whitsell, who was awarded the Wayfinder's Compass, Class III, for the discovery.

On 13 May 311 an unknown ship armed with energy weapons attacked STB-01 [[Beacon 001]] at Abernoth. Three hits pierced her hull and crippled the stabilisation module, and the Beacon suffered a catastrophic failure and exploded. Her commander, [[Octavian Paccius]], was lost with the ship. Lifepods carried 54 of her 250 crew clear, and 196 died. Something still hunts at Abernoth, among the old wrecks. It was the Accord's first ship lost to a hostile hand, and no one knows whose hand it was.

The 7th Abernoth Reaction Force came to hunt. On 30 May 311 the heavy cruiser Orvaux and the light cruiser Aubrac held the jump point where Beacon 001 died, while two strike groups swept the dark between the two jump points. They found nothing. The enemy struck instead at the salvager [[Afterglow 002]], making her slow retreat out of the system 116 million km and five hours from the nearest warship. In little more than a minute of fire from eight energy guns that showed no hull, she was destroyed. Her commander, [[Laban Greenbrook]], was lost with her, and 147 of her 225 crew died. In seventeen days the ghosts of Abernoth cost the Accord two ships and 343 lives.

On 27 June 311 the Reaction Force returned to Aurane after a fruitless sweep. The Council made sensor research the Lyceum's first priority, and until the realm can see its enemy, Abernoth is left to the ghosts.

On 1 August 311 the Lyceum delivered its finding: the enemy is not invisible, but small, and it sees before it is seen. The Yards were ordered to build new eyes, a great thermal sensor to catch a hunter's engines and an active sensor tuned for small craft, for a picket that will sail dark.
