Home to the ruins of a destroyed alien outpost, the first alien ruins found by the Accord: someone built here before us, and someone tore it down.

SV-03 Unmapped found them on 23 August 305, under Sub-Lieutenant Earnest Stilldale, who was awarded the Archaeologist, Class II. As the system's best colony prospect, Nyrene II gives the Antiquaries a reason to come.
