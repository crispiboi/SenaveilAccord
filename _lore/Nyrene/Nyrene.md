The youngest true star yet charted and the brightest of the red dwarfs, with a crowded court stretching out to 3.66 billion km.

Found on 6 May 305 by SV-04 Far Ledger under Sub-Lieutenant Selma Lumiranta. Nyrene II is the second-best colony prospect found so far, after [[Quarrane]] IV.

On 23 August 305 SV-03 Unmapped found the ruins of a destroyed alien outpost on [[Nyrene II]], the first alien ruins found by the Accord.
