The brightest of the three stars reached so far, and the first with something worth claiming.

Found on 15 August 301 by SV-01 Driftmark under Sub-Lieutenant Herbert Whitsell. Dorrane III is the best prospect found beyond Aurane so far, a large world whose ammonia is probably what holds its colony cost at 2.00.

The gravitational survey of Dorrane was completed on 19 September 307.
