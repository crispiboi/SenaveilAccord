Rebuilt after the losses at [[Abernoth]], when the Lyceum concluded that the enemy is small and sees first. The 2A trades the old thermal sensor for the Institutum Quell TH-80 Great Thermal Sensor, to catch a hunter's engine heat at long range, and adds the Lumme Series 540 R5 active sensor, which can find anything of 250 t or more and is kept dark until the thermal has a contact. Three Atelier Montclair F-35-27 magneto-plasma drives replace four Ion drives, for more speed from fewer engines.
