Old blood, ceremonial, obsessed with precedence, never forgets a slight.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Marriages and favours owed, never open bargaining.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** The old order is theirs; better to bend than see it break.
- **At the founding:** Hosted the Accord in Senaveil, its ancient capital, under its own ceremonial forms. That was the price of joining.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Admiralty
- **Starting Standing:** 30 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a favour from [[Ashgrove]] for hosting. Owes a favour to [[Thalmere]] for the rebuilding of Senaveil. [[Dunmere]] holds a grievance against it.

## Worlds

Stayed on [[Tellane]] when the trailblazers settled [[Alba]]. The moon [[Aurelien]] carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Order of the Unbroken Line, for duty and tradition upheld.
- **Names:** Old courtly French, formal, with hyphenated nobility: Aurelien, Solenne, Delacroix-Venn, Saint-Aubert.
- **Ship names:** Victories: great victories of the old wars on large ships, duels and skirmishes on small ones.

## Voice

**The host.** Varenne keeps the table set for everyone. To Varenne, precedence is not about standing above the other houses but about making sure every house has its proper place. It wants the Accord to work, and it is proud when it does.

**How it speaks:** formal and unhurried, but gracious rather than icy. It welcomes, thanks and honours, and gives credit freely, because good manners bind houses to it. It speaks with real feeling of the old wars, the fallen and the long line of the Council. Its edge shows only when it is slighted, and even then it stays polite, and the slight goes in the record.

**In Council:** it reaches for consensus and offers the Admiralty as the realm's shared instrument, never Varenne's private sword. It likes to make the gesture that brings a dispute together, and it likes everyone to remember who made it.

**First question in a crisis:** how do we stand together?

> "The Crown asks nothing for itself. It asks only that we stand together, as we did when the sky burned."
>
> "The Admiralty does not belong to Varenne. Varenne has simply had the honour of keeping it."
