Patient and quietly ruthless.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Rarely bid openly, yet end up holding things.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Real but transactional: they remember exactly what you owe.
- **At the founding:** Paid for the rebuilding of Senaveil and quietly wrote the procedural rules.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Yards
- **Starting Standing:** 30 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a favour from [[Varenne]] for the rebuilding of Senaveil.

## Worlds

A trailblazer house. It paid to settle [[Alba]] and holds claims there, as well as living on [[Tellane]]. The moon [[Garrick]] carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** The Keel, for building something that lasts.
- **Names:** Plain, solid Anglo, with trade and craft surnames: Garrick, Rowena, Ashby, Hartsell.
- **Ship names:** Works: foundries, bridges and yards on large ships, tools on small ones.
