Principled idealists, sincere, a little sanctimonious.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Broker peace, and call rivals out in public.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** True believers in the Compact and the Council as a sacred trust.
- **At the founding:** Proposed a standing council after the last great conventional war, so disputes could be settled before they became wars.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Chancery
- **Starting Standing:** 25 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Owes a favour to [[Varenne]] for hosting. [[Oskari]] holds a grievance against it.

## Worlds

Stayed on [[Tellane]] when the trailblazers settled [[Alba]]. The moon [[Constant]] carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Covenant Medal, for peace kept and principle held under pressure.
- **Names:** Virtue names and pastoral places, earnest: Constance, Steadfast, Brightwater, Fairholm.
- **Ship names:** Virtues: cardinal virtues on large ships, small kindnesses and courtesies on small ones.

## Voice

**The conscience.** Ashgrove believes the Compact is a sacred trust, and that a principle you will not act on is no principle. It deliberates hard, but once it decides, it moves first and does not waver. Its rivals find it tiresome because it means every word it says.

**How it speaks:** earnest and moral, but short once the matter is settled. It quotes the Compact and the founding, and asks pointed questions in open session instead of making accusations. It does not hedge: it says "we will" and "we will not".

**In Council:** it argues for doing the right thing and putting safeguards into law. When it is sure, it pushes for action and takes the lead. It brokers compromises, but it will not trade away the principle at the heart of one.

**First question in a crisis:** is it right?

> "We have asked whether it is right. It is. Now we ask only how soon."
>
> "The Compact was not signed by the comfortable. It was signed by people who had watched the sky burn."
