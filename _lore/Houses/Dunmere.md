Warm, loud, populist, loyal to their own.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Champion the overlooked; better at courtly games than they admit.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Believe in the whole family of houses.
- **At the founding:** Signed last with [[Oskari]], as a junior party.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Marches
- **Starting Standing:** 25 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a grievance against [[Varenne]].

## Worlds

A trailblazer house. It paid to settle [[Alba]] and holds claims there, as well as living on [[Tellane]]. The moon [[Callum]] carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** The Hearthstone, for courage spent protecting others.
- **Names:** Scots and Gaelic, rough and warm: Callum, Eilidh, Dunbar, MacAra.
- **Ship names:** Home: homesteads and regions on large ships, folk heroes of the colony on small ones.
