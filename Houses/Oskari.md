# Oskari

[Houses](Houses.md)

Restless optimists, charming, impulsive, quick to overreach.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** First to bid on anything new, rarely keeps an office long.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Everyone likes them, though nobody trusts their follow-through.
- **At the founding:** Signed last with [Dunmere](Dunmere.md), as a junior party.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Wayfinders
- **Starting Standing:** 25 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a grievance against [Ashgrove](Ashgrove.md).

## Worlds

A trailblazer house. It paid to settle [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md) and holds claims there, as well as living on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md). The moon [Ahti](../Systems/Aurane/Ahti.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** The Far Star, for daring firsts.
- **Names:** Bright Nordic and Finnish, short and quick: Aarni, Tuuli, Halvorsen, Lumme.
- **Ship names:** Journeys: great voyages and first landings on large ships, winds on small ones.
