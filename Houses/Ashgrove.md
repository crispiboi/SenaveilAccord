# Ashgrove

[Houses](Houses.md)

Principled idealists, sincere, a little sanctimonious.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Broker peace, and call rivals out in public.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** True believers in the Compact and the Council as a sacred trust.
- **At the founding:** Proposed a standing council after the last great conventional war, so disputes could be settled before they became wars.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Chancery
- **Starting Standing:** 25 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Owes a favour to [Varenne](Varenne.md) for hosting. [Oskari](Oskari.md) holds a grievance against it.

## Worlds

Stayed on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md) when the trailblazers settled [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md). The moon [Constant](../Systems/Aurane/Constant.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Covenant Medal, for peace kept and principle held under pressure.
- **Names:** Virtue names and pastoral places, earnest: Constance, Steadfast, Brightwater, Fairholm.
- **Ship names:** Virtues: cardinal virtues on large ships, small kindnesses and courtesies on small ones.
