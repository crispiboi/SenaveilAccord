# Houses

The Senaveil Accord is a council of six great houses, founded after the last great conventional war and meeting in Senaveil, [Varenne](Varenne.md)'s ancient capital. The name means six houses under one veil, sharing one canopy of law.

## The six houses

Listed in founding order. Each house is set apart by temperament rather than a domain it owns, and any house can bid for any office.

## The founding

After the last great war, [Ashgrove](Ashgrove.md) proposed a standing council so disputes could be settled before they became wars. [Varenne](Varenne.md) made hosting it in Senaveil, under Varenne's ceremonial forms, the price of joining. [Thalmere](Thalmere.md) paid for the rebuilding and quietly wrote the procedural rules. [Corvane](Corvane.md) kept the records. [Oskari](Oskari.md) and [Dunmere](Dunmere.md) signed last, as junior parties.

## The Compact

An early Trans-Newtonian test scarred [Witness](../Systems/Aurane/Witness.md), the moon of [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md), in a near-miss. The houses extended their old ban on war to TN weapons before anyone used them on each other. Houses may compete in every way except violence against one another, and house fleets act only under Council mandate. Breach is the gravest act there is.

Rivalry moves through precedence, favours owed, marriages, honours and pointed silences. Formal feuds are the release valve: declared, witnessed, fought by champions, and recorded in the ledgers.

## Offices and Standing

Each house holds one of six contested Council offices: Admiralty, Wayfinders, Marches, Yards, Chancery and Lyceum. A house's influence is its Standing, counted in marks and recorded in the public Ledger of Senaveil, which is read once per in-game decade.

## Two worlds

All six houses live on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md). [Oskari](Oskari.md), [Dunmere](Dunmere.md) and [Thalmere](Thalmere.md) are the trailblazers who also paid to settle [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md), and the claims they hold there are the first great dispute of the Trans-Newtonian age.

## How the houses speak

Every house speaks in Council as "we", and uses its house name only for formal statements and proclamations. The quickest way to tell them apart is the question each asks first when a crisis comes.

| House | First question in a crisis |
| --- | --- |
| [Varenne](Varenne.md) | How do we stand together? |
| [Ashgrove](Ashgrove.md) | Is it right? |
| [Thalmere](Thalmere.md) | Does it add up? |
| [Corvane](Corvane.md) | What could it mean? |
| [Oskari](Oskari.md) | Who goes? |
| [Dunmere](Dunmere.md) | Who is hurt, and what do we stand to lose? |
