# Dunmere

[Houses](Houses.md)

Warm, loud, populist, loyal to their own.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Champion the overlooked; better at courtly games than they admit.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Believe in the whole family of houses.
- **At the founding:** Signed last with [Oskari](Oskari.md), as a junior party.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Marches
- **Starting Standing:** 25 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a grievance against [Varenne](Varenne.md).

## Worlds

A trailblazer house. It paid to settle [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md) and holds claims there, as well as living on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md). The moon [Callum](../Systems/Aurane/Callum.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** The Hearthstone, for courage spent protecting others.
- **Names:** Scots and Gaelic, rough and warm: Callum, Eilidh, Dunbar, MacAra.
- **Ship names:** Home: homesteads and regions on large ships, folk heroes of the colony on small ones.

## Voice

**Steel.** Dunmere is the most populist house, for the good of all. Where Oskari is brave, Dunmere is simply tough and hard-working. Apart from the old houses, it is the most rooted in the ground and in the present. It grants the others their vision, then asks who pays for it and who is minding what we already have.

**How it speaks:** loud, warm and plain. It speaks for the crews, the colonists and the people of the second world, and it names the dead rather than counting them. It is better at courtly games than it lets on.

**In Council:** it argues for the people already in the realm's care, and accepts the idealism of others only once someone has answered for what it costs at home.

**First question in a crisis:** who is hurt, and what do we stand to lose?
