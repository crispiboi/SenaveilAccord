# Corvane

[Houses](Houses.md)

Cold, curious, aloof.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Trade in information; want offices only for what they can learn.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Polite to all, close to none.
- **At the founding:** Kept the records. Its archivists keep the Ledger of Senaveil and read it aloud to the Council.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Lyceum
- **Starting Standing:** 20 marks

## Worlds

Stayed on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md) when the trailblazers settled [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md). The moon [Lucan](../Systems/Aurane/Lucan.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Order of the Owl, for knowledge won or preserved.
- **Names:** Latinate and clipped, cold and scholarly: Lucan, Octavia, Corvinus, Quell.
- **Ship names:** Knowledge: great ideas and libraries on large ships, instruments such as Astrolabe and Sextant on small ones.
