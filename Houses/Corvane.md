# Corvane

[Houses](Houses.md)

Cold, curious, aloof.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Trade in information; want offices only for what they can learn.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** Polite to all, close to none.
- **At the founding:** A newer house than the others, it signed because it had to and kept the records. Its archivists keep the Ledger of Senaveil and read it aloud to the Council.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Lyceum
- **Starting Standing:** 20 marks

## Worlds

Stayed on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md) when the trailblazers settled [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md). The moon [Lucan](../Systems/Aurane/Lucan.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Order of the Owl, for knowledge won or preserved.
- **Names:** Latinate and clipped, cold and scholarly: Lucan, Octavia, Corvinus, Quell.
- **Ship names:** Knowledge: great ideas and libraries on large ships, instruments such as Astrolabe and Sextant on small ones.

## Voice

**What could be.** The old houses argue from what has always been, and Corvane has too little of that to lean on. It signed the founding because it had to, not because it believed in it, and it treats the old ways as it treats a law of nature: it will not break them and will not pretend they can be changed, but it will take them apart to see how they work. It keeps the record to see where things are heading, not to honour the past.

**How it speaks:** cool, curious and speculative: "if", "suppose", "on present evidence". It asks the questions nobody else thought to ask, and it is never alarmed, only interested.

**In Council:** it argues for the long view, for research and for preparing for what has not happened yet. It trades in knowledge and early warnings, and has little patience for "that is not how it has been done".

**First question in a crisis:** what could it mean?

> "We do not dispute the custom. We only note that it was made for a smaller sky."
>
> "We are not afraid of the dark. We would simply like to know what is in it."
