# Varenne

[Houses](Houses.md)

Old blood, ceremonial, obsessed with precedence, never forgets a slight.

## In the Council

- **How it plays politics:** Marriages and favours owed, never open bargaining.
- **Why it keeps the peace:** The old order is theirs; better to bend than see it break.
- **At the founding:** Hosted the Accord in Senaveil, its ancient capital, under its own ceremonial forms. That was the price of joining.

## On the Ledger

- **Starting office:** Admiralty
- **Starting Standing:** 30 marks
- **Starting favours and grievances:** Holds a favour from [Ashgrove](Ashgrove.md) for hosting. Owes a favour to [Thalmere](Thalmere.md) for the rebuilding of Senaveil. [Dunmere](Dunmere.md) holds a grievance against it.

## Worlds

Stayed on [Tellane](../Systems/Aurane/Tellane.md) when the trailblazers settled [Alba](../Systems/Aurane/Alba.md). The moon [Aurelien](../Systems/Aurane/Aurelien.md) carries a founder's name from this house.

## Customs

- **House honour:** Order of the Unbroken Line, for duty and tradition upheld.
- **Names:** Old courtly French, formal, with hyphenated nobility: Aurelien, Solenne, Delacroix-Venn, Saint-Aubert.
- **Ship names:** Victories: great victories of the old wars on large ships, duels and skirmishes on small ones.
